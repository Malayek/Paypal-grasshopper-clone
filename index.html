Article: Why “Hello, World” still matters
A short story‑style article that introduces your very first JavaScript line.
Lesson article #1

Imagine you open the door of a brand‑new hotel room. There’s a welcome card on the table that simply says “Hello”. In coding, that welcome card is your Hello, World program.

Your browser is the room; JavaScript is how you talk to it. When you write one line of code and the browser replies correctly, you’ve checked in.

What you’ll do in this first lesson

  • Open the browser console – your secret control panel.
  • Type one line of JavaScript that says hello to the world.
  • See an instant response, like a friendly receptionist.

We’ll start very small on purpose. The goal is not to be clever, it’s to prove that you can speak to the computer and it listens.
